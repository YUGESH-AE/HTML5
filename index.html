Taco Trivia

when did the tacos first appear in the United states?

The earliest known mention of tacos in the U.S. press appeared in a newspaper around 1905, describing them as a street food eaten by Mexican workers. They spread quickly in Southwestern states like Texas, New Mexico, Arizona, and California, where Mexican communities were already established. By the 1920s and 1930s, tacos started appearing in U.S. cookbooks and restaurants. In 1962, Glen Bell (founder of Taco Bell) helped popularize the hard-shell taco nationwide by adapting it for fast food service.
